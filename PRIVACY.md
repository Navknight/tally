# Tally privacy policy

Tally keeps all data on your device, in one local database. It has no account, no analytics, and no network access.

- **SMS:** with your permission, Tally reads bank and UPI transaction messages to record transactions. Other messages are skipped and not stored. Nothing leaves the device.
- **Statements:** CSV or PDF files you import are read once, on the device.
- **Deletion:** uninstalling Tally, or clearing its storage, deletes everything.
