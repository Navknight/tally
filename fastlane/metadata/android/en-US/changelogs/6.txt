Redesigned throughout. Every screen now has a large title that collapses as you scroll, cards that actually read as cards, and bank logos on your accounts.

Much faster. Screens read one in-memory store instead of re-querying, so switching tabs is instant, and the lists that used to build hundreds of rows before painting now build only what you can see.

New:
- Search the ledger, and filter by account, category or amount.
- Tap a day on the week strip, or a slice of the category ring, to see exactly those transactions.
- Hold a row to select several, then label, exclude or delete them in one go. Select all works on the whole filter, not just what is loaded.
- Your own categories: rename, recolour, re-mark, add and delete. Deleting asks where its transactions should go.
- Merge two accounts when SMS detection made duplicates of one.
- Insights can look at last period, six months or all time, and at every account rather than only the budgeted ones. Plus who took the most money.
- A row that needs a label now opens straight to the categories, one tap to file it, and tells you how many other rows moved with it.
- Undo when you swipe a transaction away.
